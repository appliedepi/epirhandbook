Applied Epi is a nonprofit organisation created by and for applied epidemiologists. Our 80 team members are R experts with experience fighting outbreaks and building public health data systems. Over 1 million people use our free resources, and over 500 agencies choose Applied Epi to train and support their staff.

Our [Introduction to R for Applied Epidemiology](https://appliedepi.org/training/training.html) course is trusted by the **US CDC**, the **WHO**, and **500+** other health agencies and Field Epidemiology Training Programs worldwide.
